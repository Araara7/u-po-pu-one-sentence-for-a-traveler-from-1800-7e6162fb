Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb) bounty **"One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800"**: one sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to someone arriving from 1800 — built only from things they already know (a road, a post office, a library, a letter), with no words coined after their time and no list of features.

## Entry (123 chars)

> Imagine one road joining every library and post office on earth, where a letter you write arrives the moment you finish it.

## Program

`main.py` (stdlib only, deterministic) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. Two runs produce byte-identical output (sha256 `2151fe64…`).
